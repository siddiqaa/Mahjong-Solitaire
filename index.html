Base value per tile. Each matched pair scores double (2x).
Combo Multiplier Chain
Match another pair within 5 seconds to build your chain: ×2, ×3, ×4… Clicking blocked tiles, missing clicks, using Hints, or taking too long resets your combo chain back to ×1.
Time Penalty
Traditional wisdom values swift minds. Your final outcome score loses 1 point for each second of play. The in-game meter displays accumulating matching points; final calculations are applied when the board finishes.
Enlightenment Bonus
Successfully clear all 144 tiles from the velvet table to receive an extra completion bonus: +500 points.
Classic Hand Rules
You can match identical tiles to remove them. Flowers can match any flower, and Seasons can match any season. A tile is free (selectable) ONLY if nothing sits directly above it AND at least one long edge (left or right side) is completely open.
